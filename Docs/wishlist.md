# War game

- base it off hearts of iron 4 for large scale
- war thunder for small scale
- make a real time large scale war 
- each troop is modeled
- you can control individual troops

## setting
- 2030s - 40s
- europe, and asia

## Foundation
- world war 3
- main element: commanding
- secondary elements:  flying, troop
- control a figher plane like war thunder
- launch missiles
- fight other planes
- take off from aircraft carrier
- able to control other planes and navy 
- big explosions

## flying
- gforces
- fuel leaks
- first person cockpit view
- land and takeoff to restock

## troop control
- play as front line infantry, hiding from drones and tanks
- launch infared drones to find enemies and kill them

## command
- press m for map to move navy, army, and air force
- choose where they move and fire
- see battles on macro scale
- communicate it using radio
- fire anti air missiles
- aim and lauch warheads from mainland
- launch and gather recon from satillites you launch
- attack stragiegic areas such as bridges, power plants, and other infrastrucutre
- budget food, resources, and power
- enemies can attack your infrastructure

## large scale war
- fight on the american side
- attack russian and chinese troops and planes
- multiple battle zones over europe and japanese sea
- nato countries vs russia, china, and iraq

## technical
- bevy engine
- rust
- small prototype game (hardcoding is ok!)
